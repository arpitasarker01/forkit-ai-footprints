# Community Code of Conduct

Forkit AI Footprints is a technical community built around careful measurement,
privacy, and honest evidence. Contributors and maintainers are expected to be
respectful, constructive, and precise.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and review code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and explain technical decisions with evidence.
- Respect privacy. Never post prompts, credentials, account identity, private
  paths, customer data, or another person's device evidence.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and avoid misleading claims about accuracy,
  security, or product support.
- Accept maintainer decisions about product scope, safety, and release timing.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, deliberate privacy
violations, and repeated disruptive behavior are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report community conduct concerns to `security@forkit.dev`. Report software
security or privacy vulnerabilities privately through the repository's GitHub
Security Advisory form. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close
contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community
and users.

This policy applies in the repository and in other spaces where someone is
representing the Forkit AI Footprints community.
